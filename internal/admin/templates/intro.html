Let's get your information online

People are already asking chatbots and AI assistants questions like "is there a bed available tonight" — and those assistants often answer from old or scraped information instead of what you actually publish.

This tool takes the information you already keep — a spreadsheet, a document, or a simple text file — and keeps it available so that those assistants can find your real, current answer instead of guessing.

Setup takes two short steps: point us at your information, then tell us where to send new requests. Nothing else leaves this computer.
