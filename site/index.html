Why this demo exists

The Detroit resources page linked above blocks bots and crawlers outright — try fetching it with anything other than a browser and see for yourself. That's not a knock on the city; it's the exact problem this project exists to fix: real, current civic information that only a human clicking through a page can reach. Harbor House is a stand-in for any provider — a shelter, a pantry, a clinic — whose hours and availability should be just as reachable to someone's AI agent as to someone's browser.

This site is a demo for Answerable, which makes that possible. Ask your AI about this site to find more information.
